# Kirtan Mellows Posters

This context defines the language for creating Kirtan Mellows event posters.

## People and poster elements

**Poster maker**:
A person creating a Kirtan Mellows poster.
_Avoid_: User

**Poster**:
A visual announcement for a Kirtan Mellows event.

**Template**:
A reusable poster composition that defines the placement and hierarchy of poster elements independently of their specific images and colors.
_Avoid_: Theme

**Background image**:
The image filling the poster's background, behind its other visual elements.

**Featured image**:
The separate focal image placed within a template, such as a portrait, book, or illustration. Its shape depends on the composition; it is not necessarily circular.

**Event details**:
The event title and date, along with supporting information such as time range, venue, address, subtitle, or call to action.

**Brand elements**:
The recurring Kirtan Mellows identity and associated marks, mantra text, and footer.

**Palette**:
A coordinated set of colors used for poster typography, panels, and decorative elements.

**Decoration**:
An ornamental visual element that supplements the poster's content and brand elements.
